## Heroes (panels 1–3)

1. **Cyclone Akvan makes landfall, Odisha** — first reported 20 Nov 2026.
   Reuters, 20 Nov (crawled 06:45 UTC). Landfall near Gopalpur 07:30–08:30 IST
   (02:00–03:00 UTC), winds 70–80 km/h, gusts to 90 km/h (IMD). 298,676
   evacuated by Thursday evening; later 3.7 lakh per Chief Secretary Anu Garg —
   the image says "300,000+". 423 rescue teams, 9,341 shelters, 69 trains
   cancelled. Third cyclone on this coast in 24 days (Murasu a fortnight ago;
   Arnab last month, killed 6).
2. **Overnight missile barrage over Kyiv** — first reported 20 Nov 2026.
   Reuters, 20 Nov; Kyiv Independent. Explosions from 01:25 local; fires in
   Obolonskyi and Podilskyi districts; three Dnipro bridges damaged
   (Mayor Klitschko); air force warned ~02:00 that ballistic missiles were
   heading for Kyiv; no casualties reported yet at that time. The 19 Nov
   daytime drone attack (1 killed, Dutch/Austrian embassies damaged) is a
   different, excluded event.
3. **COP31 closing day, Antalya** — first reported 20 Nov 2026. Reuters
   19 Nov draft (144 → 80 paragraphs, no fossil-fuel mention, $120bn
   adaptation figure dropped) + closing-day reporting 20 Nov (asiatoday).
   Final day Friday: Bowen consulting delegations through the night, text due
   midnight Friday TRT, "35 by 35" electricity goal survives, 1.5°C overshoot
   admitted, activists' "missing cat" protest. No final agreement existed at
   build time — the image says "final day, no deal yet", not a final outcome.
